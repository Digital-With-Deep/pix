Render the A2UI (Agent-to-User Interface) 1.0 basic catalog's inputs and containers with PIX. The PIX catalog in `@pix-ui/a2ui` gains `TextField`, `CheckBox`, `ChoicePicker`, `Slider` and `DateTimeInput`, with values two-way bound to the data model and failed `@checks` shown on the field, plus `Image`, `Tabs`, `Modal` and `List`, and the `regex`, `length`, `numeric` and `email` validation functions. `@pix-ui/react` adds `Slider`, a token-styled native range input with a label, value display and min and max labels, and `DateField`, native date, time and date-and-time inputs with label, hint and error. `RadioGroup` gains `hint` and `error`.
