Card is the plain container behind most panels in a console: a title and one supporting line, a trailing action, a body, and a footer row for buttons. It has no opinion about its content, so it works for a summary, a short form, or a list that runs edge to edge.

<Preview name="card-overview" />

## When to use

- To group content that belongs together and can be read on its own, such as a run summary or a settings section.
- To give a short form or list a header and a footer row of actions.
- Prefer MetricCard for a single number with a delta, and PlanCard for a pricing tier; both are cards with a fixed layout.
- Don't nest cards. Use dividers or Disclosure inside one card instead.

## Variants

`padding` sets the body gutter: `sm`, `md` (the default) or `lg`. With `padding="none"` the body runs flush to the border, which suits a list or a table; the header and footer keep their gutter.

## Anatomy

- `title` — the heading, rendered as `h3` by default; set `headingLevel` to fit the page outline.
- `description` — one supporting line under the title.
- `action` — a trailing control in the header, such as a small Button, a Menu or a Badge.
- `children` — the body.
- `footer` — a right-aligned row on a subtle background, usually one or two Buttons.

## Accessibility

- With a `title`, the card renders `role="group"` named by its heading, so screen reader users hear the card's name when they move into it. Pass `role="region"` to make an important card a landmark, sparingly.
- Pick `headingLevel` so headings stay in order on the page; a card's title should sit one level below the section it lives in.
- Card itself isn't interactive. If the whole card should act as a link, put a real link in the title rather than a click handler on the surface.
- Body text and the description meet 4.5:1 in light and dark themes.
