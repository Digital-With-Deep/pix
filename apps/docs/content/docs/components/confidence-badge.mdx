ConfidenceBadge tells the reader how much weight an answer can bear. Each of its four states has its own colour, icon and label, and it can carry a score, what the confidence rests on, and a reason in a tooltip.

<Preview name="confidence-badge-states" />

## When to use

- Next to an AI answer, figure or table cell that a person might act on, so they know whether to act, check or escalate.
- With a `scope` when the confidence depends on what was checked: "checked against 3 sources".
- With a `reason` when the state is anything but confident, so the reader knows what to look at.
- Prefer Badge for statuses that are not about trust, and Callout when the reason needs a full sentence on screen.

## States

| State | Label | Colour |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `confident` | Confident | Emerald (`--confidence-high`) |
| `low` | Low confidence | Amber (`--confidence-low`) |
| `review` | Needs review | Blue |
| `failed` | Check failed | Red (`--claim`) |

`score` adds a monospace percentage (0.62 shows "62%"). `label` overrides the words, but keep them in words: the colour is never the only signal.

`size="standalone"` is a larger block for a summary panel, with the scope on its own line.

## Anatomy

- **Icon** — check, alert, eye or cross, by state.
- **Label** — the state in words, or `label`.
- **Score** — optional, monospace.
- **Scope** — optional plain-words note on what was checked.
- **Reason** — optional; shown in a tooltip on hover or focus.

## Accessibility

- The state is always written out; the icon is decorative.
- When `reason` is set, the badge joins the tab order, the reason opens on focus as a `role="tooltip"`, and screen readers get it through `aria-describedby`. Escape closes it.
- Each state's text meets 4.5:1 against its tint in light and dark.
