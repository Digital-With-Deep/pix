PatternDetail is the page for a single finding. It leads with what happens and who is affected, then gives the reader what they need to check the claim before acting on it: impact over time, a per-model comparison, example traces with the passage that matters highlighted, and source references. The Properties panel holds status, severity and categories, and the header holds the actions.

## When to use

- Beside a [PatternList](/docs/components/pattern-list), as the detail of the selected finding.
- On its own page, reached from an alert link.
- Not for a single trace. Open the trace in an [ExecutionTrace](/docs/components/execution-trace) or an [AuditLog](/docs/components/audit-log) and link to it with `onOpenTrace`.

## Check the evidence before acting

A pattern is a claim made by whatever analysed the traces. The component is built so the claim can be checked:

- **Evidence** lists example traces. `kind` says what each one shows: the `problem`, a `retry` that succeeded, or `contrast` behaviour that helps explain the finding. `highlight` marks a passage of the `excerpt`, and "Show reason" reveals why that passage supports the finding.
- The **Code** tab lists source references with file, lines and revision. Links render only for http(s) and relative URLs.
- **`cause`** labels the suggested fix as `confirmed` by evidence or as a `hypothesis` to confirm before acting. Set it honestly: a suggested fix without supporting evidence should say what to check, not what to change.
- **Models** compares each model's share of the pattern's matches with its share of all traffic, and flags a model whose match share is at least 1.5 times its traffic share.

## Act on a pattern

- **Investigate** appears with `onInvestigate`. Start a deeper investigation with the pattern attached, and set the status to `investigating` while it runs.
- **Copy as prompt** copies a Markdown brief for a coding agent: the finding, impact, suggested fix, evidence and source references. With `traceCommand` it adds one command per evidence trace, replacing `{{trace}}` with the trace id. `patternPrompt(pattern, options)` builds the same brief without the button.
- **Copy link** appears with `href`.
- **Properties** become editable when you pass `onStatusChange`, `onSeverityChange` and `onCategoriesChange`. Choosing a new status or severity opens an optional reason box; the callback fires when the reader presses Apply. Record the change and its reason in `activity`, where the next reader will look for it.
- **Is this pattern useful?** appears with `onRate` while the pattern has no `rating`. No says that it dismisses and closes the pattern; close it in your handler.

## Impact over time

`impactSeries` draws one bar per period. When more than one unit has data the reader can count by traces, users or sessions, and when every point has a `total` they can switch between counts and percentages. With `onOpenPeriod` each bar is a button that opens the matching logs for that period.

## Anatomy

```tsx
<PatternDetail
  pattern={finding}
  href={`https://app.example.com/patterns/${finding.id}`}
  traceCommand="traces get {{trace}} --format json"
  onInvestigate={startInvestigation}
  onStatusChange={(status, reason) => save({ status }, reason)}
  onSeverityChange={(severity, reason) => save({ severity }, reason)}
  onRate={(rating, comment) => rate(finding.id, rating, comment)}
  onOpenTrace={(evidence) => openTrace(evidence.traceId)}
/>
```

The component is controlled: it shows `pattern` and reports intent. Update the pattern in your handlers.

## Accessibility

- The finding is an `article` named by the pattern, with the name as its heading.
- Evidence is a tablist of Traces and Code; arrow keys move between the two tabs.
- "Show reason" is a button with `aria-expanded` that controls the reason text.
- Each bar of the impact chart has a text label with its period, value and unit. As a button it adds "Open matching logs".
- Copying announces "Prompt copied" or "Link copied" through a live region.
- The highlighted passage is a `mark` with an underline as well as a tint, so it does not rely on colour.
