Popover opens a panel next to the element you pass as its child. It's non-modal: the rest of the page stays usable, and the panel closes on a click outside, on Escape or when focus leaves it.

<Preview name="popover-overview" />

## When to use

- To explain a value or a setting in more detail than a Tooltip allows, including links.
- For a quick edit of one or two fields next to the thing they change.
- Prefer Tooltip for a short text label with nothing to interact with.
- Prefer Menu for a list of commands, and Dialog when the user must finish or cancel before going on.

## Variants

`placement` opens the panel below (the default) or above the trigger, and `align` lines it up with the trigger's `start`, `center` or `end` edge. Control it with `open` and `onOpenChange` when an action inside should close it, as the form below does on submit.

## Anatomy

- `children` — the trigger, usually a Button. Clicking it toggles the panel.
- `title` — an optional heading at the top of the panel; it also names the panel.
- `content` — what the panel holds.
- `width` — the panel width, 280px by default.

## Accessibility

- The trigger gets `aria-haspopup="dialog"`, `aria-expanded` and, while open, `aria-controls`. The panel has `role="dialog"` and is named by `title`, or by `label` when there's no visible title.
- On open, focus moves to the first focusable element in the panel, or the panel itself. Set `initialFocus={false}` to leave focus on the trigger.
- Escape closes the panel and returns focus to the trigger. Tabbing out of the panel closes it.
- Keep content short. Anything the user must complete belongs in a Dialog, where focus is held.
