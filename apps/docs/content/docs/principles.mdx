---
title: Design principles
description: The rules the system holds itself to.
---

1. **Legible over impressive.** Verdicts in words, identifiers in monospace.
2. **Show the work.** Reasoning, tool calls and sources sit one click from every answer.
3. **Claim ≠ truth.** What a model said (`--claim`) and the verified answer (`--truth`) never share a colour.
4. **Calm chrome.** Warm neutrals, one accent used sparingly, sentence case, no emoji.
5. **Accessible by default.** 4.5:1 text contrast in both themes, keyboard reachable, reduced-motion respected.
